## 1. Store Metadata / 商店基础信息

### Chinese (简体中文)

* **App Name (应用名称)**: StudyLoop - 柯基伴侣与专注起步助手
* **Short Description (简短介绍, Max 80 chars)**: 
  识别学习阻力，拆解第一步。带一只温暖的柯基小狗，陪你诚实开启每次专注。
* **Full Description (完整介绍)**:
  【为什么我知道该学习，却总是开始不了？】
  很多时候，无法开始并不是因为缺乏自制力，而是因为启动阻力太大：面对复杂的作业无从下手、复习任务堆积如山、手机通知频繁打扰、或者因精力见底而无法强撑。

  StudyLoop 专为面对学业压力的大学生设计。它不是冰冷的任务打卡器，而是一位温和、实事求是的起步伴侣。

  【核心功能特色】
  1. 🎯 5 维学习阻力识别：
     在每次开始前，先轻点选出当前最大阻力（无从下手、任务太多、手机干扰、精力不足、过度准备），针对性降低心智负荷。
  2. ⚡ 即时极小行动拆解（Minimum Action）：
     拒绝宏大的假想目标，将任务自动缩小为 10~15 分钟即可迈出的第一步（例如“只写代码骨架”、“只做前 2 道计算题”）。
  3. 🐶 温暖柯基 AI 学习伴侣：
     一只安静陪伴你的柯基小狗。向它倾诉学业困惑，它会帮你把任务拆小、提供温暖回应。
  4. ⏱️ 诚实计时与零评判复盘：
     仅统计真实前台专注时间；支持随时提前结束，绝不给予惩罚。复盘支持自由跳过，拒绝虚假评分。
  5. 📊 本地优先规律洞察：
     积累真实学习记录后，本地分析你的最佳起步时长、高效时段与任务类型分布。核心闭环永久免费，数据保存在设备本地，无需注册。

* **Category (应用分类)**: 教育 (Education) / 效率 (Productivity)
* **Target Audience (目标人群)**: 大学生、备考青年、科研工作者

### English (英文)

* **App Name**: StudyLoop: Focus Start Companion
* **Short Description (Max 80 chars)**:
  Overcome starting friction, break tasks into tiny steps, and focus with your Corgi.
* **Full Description**:
  "I know I need to study, so why can't I just start?"

  Friction often comes before procrastination. When a project feels overwhelming, directions are ambiguous, or phone notifications buzz constantly, getting into the chair is the hardest part.

  StudyLoop is a calm, local-first study start companion built for university students. Rather than demanding perfection, it helps you identify friction and take an honest, doable first step.

  KEY FEATURES:
  1. Identify 5 Core Barriers:
     Recognize what holds you back before opening books: Unsure where to start, Overload, Phone distraction, Tiredness, or Over-preparation.
  2. Concrete Micro-Action Generation:
     Shrink intimidating projects into immediate 10–15 minute starting steps.
  3. Corgi AI Learning Companion:
     A calm, supportive Corgi who stays by your side, understands your context, and offers practical micro-steps.
  4. Honest Timer & Judgment-Free Reflection:
     Foreground-only timer that honors your real time. Ending early is completely fine. Reflections can be answered or skipped without falsifying data.
  5. Local-First Insights:
     Discover your optimal focus duration, peak hours, and task mix. 100% on-device SQLite storage. No account required.

* **Category**: Education / Productivity
* **Content Rating**: Everyone

## 2. Privacy & Subscription Summary (合规与订阅说明)

* **Data Safety (数据安全说明)**:
  * No user registration or identity binding.
  * Local study history and reflections are stored on-device in SQLite.
  * AI Companion sends only the current message and minimal 7-day aggregated counts. Full database dumps are prohibited.
  * Purchases processed securely via RevenueCat and Google Play Billing.
* **Subscription (Pro 订阅说明)**:
  * Free Tier: Complete study loop, immediate starting actions, focus timer, companion, reflection, and short-term insights are forever free.
  * Pro Tier: Unlocks multi-month trends, cross-barrier pattern comparisons, and deep category analysis.
* **Medical Disclaimer (非医疗声明)**:
  * StudyLoop does not provide clinical diagnosis or treatment for ADHD, anxiety, or depressive disorders.
